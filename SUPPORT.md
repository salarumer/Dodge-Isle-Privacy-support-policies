## Frequently asked questions

**Which iPhones can play Dodge Isle?**
iPhone 11 and newer, running iOS 26 or later.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. Dodge Isle works fully offline.

**Where is my progress saved? Will I lose it?**
Coins, unlocked characters, skins, themes, lands and your best score are saved on your iPhone. They stay when you close the app or restart your phone. Deleting the app erases them.

**How do I unlock more characters, skins, themes and lands?**
Collect coins while you play, then spend them: tap your character on the home screen for characters and skins, open ⚙ Settings for themes and lands, or drag the map to buy lands from their signs.

**How do I change the controls?**
Open ⚙ Settings → Controls. There are five styles, and you can put the stick on the left or right.

**The game feels too hard (or too easy).**
Open ⚙ Settings → Difficulty and choose Easy, Medium or Hard.

**I want to start over.**
Open ⚙ Settings → Danger Zone → Reset Everything. This erases your coins, unlocks and best score.

**The game crashed or something looks wrong.**
Please email me with the details above. Making sure you're on the latest version of the app and iOS also helps.
