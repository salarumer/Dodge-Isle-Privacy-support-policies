# Dodge Isle — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Dodge Isle ("the app") is a single-player game for iPhone, made by Salar Muhammad Umer.

## We collect no data

The app does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app works entirely offline.

## What stays on your device

Your game progress — coins, unlocked characters, skins, themes and lands, best score, and settings such as controls and sound — is saved only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal data.

## Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
